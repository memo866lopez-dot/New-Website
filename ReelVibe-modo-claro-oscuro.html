How to use it

1. Choose your video

Upload a local video or paste a direct HTTP/HTTPS video URL and press Load. Streaming platforms and ordinary web pages are not direct media URLs. Wait for the ready message. Local sources are recommended for transcription, audio separation and export.

2. Play, seek, loop and trim

Play starts playback; Pause holds the current position; Stop returns to the trim start; Restart plays again from that point. The timeline seeks within your segment. Both Loop controls toggle repetition. Enter trim start/end in seconds, mark the current position with Mark start/end, reset the full clip, or apply the trim to the preview. Trimming does not change the original file.

3. Frame and mix audio

Fill frame crops to 9:16; Fit entire video preserves the full image with black bars when needed. Set Original volume and Added audio volume separately. Upload audio or load a direct audio URL; trim it using its start/end fields and mark/reset buttons. Added audio starts at the video trim start and stops at its own trim end. Remove audio clears only the added track.

4. Separate audio

Separate and download audio works with a local video. It records the selected audio in real time, requests a download, adds the result as the extra track if the video and trim have not changed, and mutes the original to avoid duplication. Cancel separation stops the operation. This is not lossless extraction and silent videos produce silence.

5. Dictate or edit text

Choose the spoken language, then Transcribe microphone to dictate and Stop transcription to finish. This listens to your microphone, not the video, and may use an online browser service. Edit transcription focuses the editable box; you can type or paste text. The interface language is separate from the recognition language.

6. Style and position captions

Choose font, weight, size and color. Use position presets or the Horizontal/Vertical sliders. Drag the caption with a mouse, pen or touch, or focus it and use arrow keys (Shift moves farther). Toggle Text background and Safe-area guide. Guides are approximate; keep long captions inside the frame.

7. Transcribe video and edit timed subtitles

Automatic transcription reads the original audio of the selected video segment, not the added track or microphone. Choose the spoken language first. The initial run downloads libraries and a multilingual Whisper model; inference runs locally and may take time or use substantial memory. Review the result. Add Subtitle creates a cue; edit its text and relative start/end times, View jumps to it, and Delete removes it. Enable timed subtitles to show cues at their timestamps. Typing in the general transcription turns off timed mode without deleting cues. After changing the video or trim, check timings or transcribe again. SRT subtitles downloads timestamps relative to the trim start, not a video.

8. Export the finished Reel

Download MP4 records the chosen segment at 720 × 1280 with framing, visible captions and both volume-adjusted audio tracks. Recording is in real time: keep the tab visible and wait for progress to finish. Cancel process stops export or transcription; model cancellation may not be immediate. The browser must support canvas capture, Web Audio and genuine MP4 recording. External media needs CORS permission. The page never disguises WebM as MP4. A download request is not proof that your browser saved the file; check your downloads.

9. Language and saving

Use Language at the top to switch all built-in interface text, help, policies, accessible labels and application messages between English and Spanish. Your video, entered captions, transcripts, filenames and URLs are preserved, not automatically translated. Changing interface language does not change the spoken-language selector. There is no project autosave: export before closing or reloading.
